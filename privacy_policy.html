2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide daily competitive tournament gaming, secure player authentication, live leaderboards, and optional rewarded ad bonuses.

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Details: When you register via Email & Password or sign in using Google Sign-In, we store your Display Name, Email address, unique User ID (UID), and profile picture URL (if provided via Google).
  • Authentication Credentials: Passwords are encrypted and securely hashed by Google Firebase Authentication. We never store or view your plain-text password.

B. Automatically Collected Gameplay & Technical Data

  • Competition & Score Data: Official daily tournament attempt counts, scores submitted per game (Tap Speed, Car Endless, Flappy, Jump & Run), timestamps of completion, and leaderboard rankings.
  • Device Identifiers & Diagnostics: Android OS version, device model, and internet connectivity state to verify fair play and prevent clock-manipulation cheating.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID) collected by the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK to deliver sponsored rewarded video ads and limit ad frequency.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To authenticate your identity across sessions and synchronize your competitive scores.
  • To enforce the 1 Official Daily Attempt tournament rule fairly for all players globally.
  • To generate and display live Top 50 Global Leaderboards.
  • To provide optional extra game run time and crash revivals when you choose to watch Rewarded Ads.
  • To detect, prevent, and mitigate fraud, automated bots, and anti-cheat rule violations.
  • To comply with legal obligations and Google Play Developer Program policies.

5. Account & Data Deletion (User Control)

Your Right to Erase Data: In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy and global privacy laws (GDPR/CCPA), you have full right to permanently delete your account and all associated personal records at any time.

You can execute complete account and data deletion in two ways:

  1. Instantly in the App:
    Navigate to Profile → Account Actions → DELETE ACCOUNT. Once confirmed, your Firebase user authentication account, profile records, and stored tournament records are permanently deleted immediately.
  2. By Email Request:
    You may email us at kashyap10102007@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - SkillPlay Arena" from your registered email. We will permanently delete your records within 48 hours.

6. Advertising & Rewarded Content

Our app incorporates Google AdMob to display rewarded video ads. Watching these ads is entirely voluntary and provides in-game benefits (such as extra seconds in Tap Speed or a shield revival in Endless games). AdMob may use your advertising ID to serve relevant ads according to your device's personalized advertising settings.

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt-out of personalized ads at any time through your Android device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID.
