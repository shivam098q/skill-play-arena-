Account & Data Deletion

App Name: SkillPlay Arena | Developer Contact: kashyap10102007@gmail.com

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies regarding User Data & Account Deletion, all users of SkillPlay Arena have the unequivocal right to permanently delete their account, gameplay scores, and all associated personal data.

Method 1: Delete Directly Inside the App (Instant)

If you have the SkillPlay Arena app installed on your device, you can delete your account instantly:

  1. Open the SkillPlay Arena app.
  2. Tap the Profile icon in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll to the ACCOUNT ACTIONS section at the bottom.
  4. Tap DELETE ACCOUNT.
  5. Review the confirmation dialog: "Delete your account? Deleting your account is permanent..."
  6. Tap Delete Account to confirm.
  7. If security re-authentication is required, confirm your password or Google account.
  8. Your account and data are immediately purged from our servers and you will be signed out.

What Data Is Deleted?

The following data is permanently erased upon account deletion:
  • Firebase Authentication record (User ID / UID, email address, password hash, and auth tokens)
  • Cloud Firestore player profile document (users/{uid})
  • All personal leaderboard scores, records, and podium ranks across all games (leaderboards/{gameId}/players/{uid})
  • All daily play attempt logs and reset timestamps (gameAttempts)
  • Locally cached session preferences and tokens

Retention & Processing Timeline

  • In-App Deletion: Processed instantly and permanently at the time of user confirmation.
  • Email Deletion Requests: Acknowledged within 48 hours and completely purged within 30 days of verification.
  • Post-Deletion Retention: Zero personal data is retained following completion of the deletion process.
